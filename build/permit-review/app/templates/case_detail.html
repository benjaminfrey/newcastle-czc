Clock Extensions & Overrides

Article 7 §6.e.1: upon mutual agreement by the applicant and the Permitting Authority, a hearing-commencement or decision time limit may be extended; §6.e.2 requires the agreement recorded in writing. §8.d.1's auto-approval trigger is itself qualified “…within the maximum time requirement or permitted extensions, as applicable” — a lawfully extended clock is recorded here so the Deadlines table above reflects the real, extended due date rather than a false auto-approval alarm. See DECISIONS-NEEDED.md D-0022 (extends vs. tolls — the conservative reading implemented), D-0024 (which clocks §6.e.1 reaches).
