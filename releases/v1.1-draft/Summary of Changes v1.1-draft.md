# Summary of Changes — v1.1-draft

v1.1-draft, October 12, 2026. Changes are stated against v1.0, the Code as most recently adopted.

This draft changes how the Code is printed, not what it says. No standard, definition, use, district, table entry, Thoroughfare Type or inventory entry has changed since the Code was adopted at the Special Town Meeting on September 14, 2026.

## Layout-only changes (a person's call)

- Article 1, General Standards: The district map pages are now bound on the correct side of the page, so the inside margin falls at the binding as in the 2020 Code. No map, label or wording changed. (decided by Ben Frey)
- Article 2, District Standards: The district pages are now bound on the correct side of the page, and the first district page (D1) again shows its page number, running head and Article tab, which were missing from the adopted edition. No district standard, use, or table entry changed. (decided by Ben Frey)
- Article 3, Thoroughfares: The Thoroughfare Type plates, the Inventory of Existing Thoroughfares (Exhibit 3.1) and the Thoroughfare Type Map (Exhibit 3.2) are now bound on the correct side of the page. The footer now reads 'Adopted: September 14, 2026' instead of an old draft label. No standard, Type assignment or inventory entry changed. (decided by Ben Frey)

The same page-side correction applies to every page of the Code: odd-numbered pages are now bound on the left and even-numbered pages on the right, as in the 2020 Code. In the adopted edition, many pages had their wider inside margin on the wrong edge. The Code still runs to 117 pages, and no text moved from one page to another.

## What is new

Nothing. No section, table, figure or definition was added.

## What changed

No wording changed. The redline therefore shows no marks. Every change in this draft is a layout change, listed above.

## What the redline cannot show

- In Article 1, the District Maps are generated pages. The redline shows them as they now read, unmarked.
- In Article 2, the thirteen district pages (district standards and use tables) are generated pages. The redline shows them as they now read, unmarked.
- In Article 3, the ten Thoroughfare Type pages, the Inventory of Existing Thoroughfares (Exhibit 3.1) and the Thoroughfare Type Map (Exhibit 3.2) are generated pages. The redline shows them as they now read, unmarked.
