# Summary of Changes — v0.11-draft

**Release type:** **Presentation + minor additive — it re-lays-out the ten Street/Road Type pages to match the Article 2 District pages, and adds a statement of Purpose to each. No regulatory standard, dimension, table value, or cross-reference changes.** Through v0.10 each Type page (S1–S5, R1–R5) carried its content in an ad-hoc order — a context kicker, the description, a blue side-bar note, then the cross-section graphic, then the Design Standards table mid-page, then two reference columns. The District pages of Article 2 use a settled grammar (gray under-ruled panel headings; **DESCRIPTION → PURPOSE →** standards panels; the full-width matrix at the bottom). This release **rebuilds every Type page to that same grammar** so a Type page reads as a torn-out page of the same Code, and **adds the missing PURPOSE section** the CZC convention calls for. It also **clarifies the cross-section's right-of-way caption** — the cryptic `(47 ft typ.)` becomes `40–54 ft range · shown at 47 ft`, with breathing room above it — and renders **every cross-section at a uniform full-column width**: each Type's section is centered in a fixed-aspect frame (the narrow Alley and the wide Main Street alike), with the streetscape (Streets) or landscape (Roads) extended into the flanks so no page reads as a section marooned in empty sky. The only regulatory-text edit is a one-sentence sync in Article 3 §2.c.5 (the inventory of what each Type page states).

**Compares against:** [v0.10-draft](../v0.10-draft/). The redesign is **structurally inert**: every page count, page position, and parity offset is **identical to v0.10** (integrated 115 pp; standalone 20 pp; the ten plates remain a 10-page even block). Nothing downstream shifts. The change is entirely *within* the ten plate pages.

**Why this release exists.** Raised in review: the Type pages and the District pages presented the same *kind* of content (an identity band, a description, a statement of applicable standards, a data table) in two different visual orders, which read as haphazard across the document. Form-based codes live or die on looking authoritative and consistent. Aligning the two page families to one layout grammar — and giving each Type the statement-of-Purpose every District already has — makes Article 3 feel like part of the same book as Article 2. The Type pages also lacked a Purpose entirely, an omission relative to the CZC's own section-by-section convention.

## 2. The new page layout

Top to bottom, every Type page now reads:

1. **Identity band** — the code badge (e.g. `S1`) + name banner (`MAIN STREET`), in the Type family's color (Streets blue, Roads olive). Unchanged.
2. **Cross-section graphic** — the Streetmix-style illustration, now the page's hero directly under the band, with its CC BY-SA credit caption beneath. The right-of-way dimension caption under the section was reworded for clarity — `RIGHT-OF-WAY 40–54 ft range · shown at 47 ft` (was the cryptic `(47 ft typ.)`) — and given clear vertical separation from the dimension bracket above it; it auto-shrinks on narrow Types (the Alley) so it never clips its canvas. Every section is also rendered at a **uniform full-column width**: it is centered in a fixed-aspect frame, with the streetscape (Streets) or landscape (Roads) extended into the flanks, so a narrow Type fills the column to the same width as a wide one — undistorted, and at the same page height.
3. **Two prose columns**, in the District-page panel grammar (gray bold under-ruled headings):
   - **Left:** `DESCRIPTION` (the authoritative one-paragraph regulatory text) → `PURPOSE` (three "To …" statements).
   - **Right:** `STANDARDS` (the Type-specific applicability statements, numbered) → `TARGET DISTRICTS` (where the Type applies) → `CHARACTER` (its qualitative attributes).
4. **DESIGN STANDARDS table** — full-width at the bottom (the analog of the District PERMITTED BUILDINGS matrix): the three-column *item / Type standard / Build-To* table, with the §3.C reading convention as a caption beneath it.

This is the same skeleton as an Article 2 District standards page — band, then a two-column body of under-ruled panels, then the page's widest data table last — so the two page families now look like one system.

## 3. The new PURPOSE statements (newly authored — flagged for review)

Each Type gained a three-item statement of Purpose, written in the CZC's "To …" voice and grounded in that Type's description, transect context, and character. **These are newly drafted interpretive prose — not derived from the repealed RDEO or the baseline — and should be reviewed by the drafter / Planning Board** for tone and policy fit, exactly as the District PURPOSE sections were. They state intent; they impose no new standard. Examples:

- **S1 Main Street** — establish the Town Center's principal civic/commercial thoroughfare; give pedestrians clear priority (lowest design speed, §1.a.4); complete the public realm with wide sidewalks, parking both sides, and street trees, served from the rear by a Lane or Alley.
- **S2 Village Street** — support a compact, walkable village center; resolve vehicle/pedestrian conflict for the pedestrian (§1.a.4); frame the realm with shallow setbacks, street trees, and entrances meeting the sidewalk.
- **R2 Rural Road** — provide reliable access to working rural landscapes with minimal land alteration; keep the road narrow and unembellished, favoring the vehicle and rural character (§1.a.4); limit impervious surface and preserve the open, ditch-drained rural character.
- **R4 Highway Commercial** — accommodate auto-oriented commercial development on the MaineDOT-maintained Route 1 cartway; secure pedestrian access and coherent frontage where feasible via Town control of setbacks/sidewalks/access (Article 2); manage curb cuts in coordination with MaineDOT (§12).

The full set (10 Types × 3 statements) lives in `source/exhibits/cross-sections/types.json` under each Type's new `purpose` key.

## 4. The STANDARDS section (relocated, not new)

The applicability statements that previously sat in a blue side-bar (`note`) and under the table (`footnote`) are **existing content**, now gathered under a single numbered `STANDARDS` heading — the District-standards-section treatment. Nothing was added or removed; the shopfront-designation note, the on-street-parking-where-retail rule, and the per-Type Road-family / MaineDOT notes read exactly as before, just in one place styled like the rest of the Code. The §3.C build-to reading convention (the fuller value for pedestrian/landscape components, the lower value for traffic-calming dimensions; "where …" components per §3.F; the Board's §6.D latitude) remains, moved to a caption directly beneath the Design Standards table — mirroring the USE TABLE LEGEND that captions the District use matrix.

## 5. The one regulatory-text edit — §2.c.5

`source/article-03-streets-roads-driveways.md` §2.c.5 inventories what each Type page states. It read "…that Type's **description, target Districts, character, and design standards**…"; it now reads "…that Type's **description, purpose, applicable standards, target Districts, character, and design standards**…", so the prose matches the redesigned pages. This is the only markdown change in the release, and the only line the text redline reports (§7).

## 6. Build integration & parity

No build-script change was needed. The Type plates were already spliced into Article 3 §2 between the General subsection (§2.c) and the Driveway subsection (§2.d) via `build/split-article-03.py`, rendered from `source/cross-section-plates.typ`, and threaded the cumulative even `page_offset`. The redesign changed only the *internals* of each one-page plate, so:

- **Integrated:** plates render at offset 38 (even) → 48, a **10-page block**, exactly as in v0.10. Every Article-opener page is unmoved; the whole document is 115 pp.
- **Standalone:** opener (offset 0→2) + plates (offset 2→12, 10 pages) + continuation (offset 12→20); 20 pp, unchanged.
- **Verso/recto correctness** depends only on the offset being even, which is unchanged — spot-checked S1 (recto) in context and R2 (verso) standalone, badges on the correct fore-edge.

## 7. Redlines

Both redlines use the project's **text-diff digest** (introduced in the v0.10 cycle): it compares the *text* of the integrated markdown and emits a compact vector PDF of only the changed passages. Layout and native-Typst content (the plate redesign, the new PURPOSE/STANDARDS rendering) live in `.typ`/JSON, **not** the markdown, so by construction they do not appear — which is exactly why this layout-heavy release produces a near-empty redline.

- **`Redline — Full CZC v0.11-draft vs v0.10-draft.pdf`** *(committed; 36 KiB)* — **one changed passage**: the §2.c.5 sentence of §5 above (`…description, ` ~~`target Districts`~~ → `purpose, applicable standards, target Districts` `…`). This is the correct and complete textual delta; the entire Type-page redesign is intentionally invisible to a *text* diff because it is layout + native-Typst content. A one-page PDF.
- **`Redline — Full CZC v0.11-draft vs v0.1-baseline.pdf`** *(committed; 1.23 MiB)* — the cumulative redline against the original baseline: 265 changed passages, unchanged in magnitude from v0.10-vs-baseline because Article 3 is wholly new relative to the baseline (the §2.c.5 tweak is absorbed into Article 3's existing all-new content). Drops the migrated District spreads from the comparison, per the v0.10-cycle redline behavior.

Both are compact vector PDFs — a world away from the 60–110 MiB raster overlays this mechanism replaced.

- **`source/cross-section-plates.typ`** — `plate()` rewritten to the District-page grammar: cross-section moved directly under the band; a two-column prose body of under-ruled panels (`DESCRIPTION` + `PURPOSE` left; `STANDARDS` + `TARGET DISTRICTS` + `CHARACTER` right); the `DESIGN STANDARDS` table moved to the bottom full-width, with the §3.C reading convention as its caption. ## 11. Notes & carry-forward

- **PURPOSE statements are newly authored.** They state intent, not new standards, but they are original interpretive prose and warrant a drafter/Planning-Board read for tone and policy fit (§3).
- **Unused `context` field.** The per-Type `context` string (e.g. "D5 Village Business · edges of D6 · SD-Historic core") fed the now-removed kicker and is no longer rendered. It is retained in `types.json` as harmless documentation; it can be pruned in a later data-cleanup pass.
- **Uniformity pass — further candidates.** This release unifies the Article 3 Type pages with the Article 2 District pages. If desired, the same layout-grammar audit could extend to other native-Typst or markdown units for a fully consistent house style.
- **Carry-forward items** from v0.10 are unchanged (the three District Map exhibits and the two presentation refinements shipped in v0.10; the Comp-Plan citation re-verification in §3.D; the unadopted ROW memo; the `article-02.typ` running-head lag; the existing-street inventory; Article 2 per-District §3.F pointers; the deferred transit-stop sidewalk trigger; the dropped Option E parking incentive). None is affected by this release.
