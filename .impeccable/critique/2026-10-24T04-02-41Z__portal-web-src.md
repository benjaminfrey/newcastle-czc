The shell and controls are Apple's Settings grammar on purpose, as the chair directed. The product's own character lives in the seal, the parcel outline on the named road, the map-backed "Here's what we found", the lot-diagram and compass layouts, and "Why we ask" tied to Code sections. ## What's Working

1. Map-backed pre-fill with honest provenance ("from the Town's maps — staff confirm").
2. "I don't know yet", the follow-up dot and Still to answer: not knowing is a recorded state, not a dead end.
3. Navigation faithful to the brief: sidebar with checkmarks on desktop, a push stack on phone, sheets lifted by tone in dark mode.
