# MEMORANDUM

**To:** Town Counsel  *(cc: Newcastle Code Enforcement Officer; Planning Board)*
**From:** Ben Frey  *(Core Zoning Code amendment drafter)*
**Date:** June 9, 2026
**Re:** "Character" language in the Variance approval standards (Article 8 §19.d) — retained verbatim as statutory mirror; confirmation requested
**Status:** **Open — counsel review requested.** The flagged language is **retained verbatim** in the draft Code (v0.20-draft). No change has been made; this memo records *why* it was left untouched when the surrounding term was cleaned up, and asks counsel to confirm.

---

## 1. Background

Draft **v0.20-draft** added a definition of **"Character"** to Article 9 and replaced the word elsewhere in the Code wherever it was doing *operative* work (classification tests and discretionary approval criteria), substituting concrete form-based terms ("built form," "development pattern," "nature"). The goal was to remove a vague, conclusory term from the binding standards.

**Two variance provisions were deliberately left alone**, because they appear to be a verbatim recital of the Maine variance statute and an ordinance must conform to its enabling act:

- **Art. 8 §19.d.3.c (Undue Hardship Variance):**
  > "The granting of a variance will not alter the essential **character** of the locality; and"

- **Art. 8 §19.d.4.b (Practical Difficulty Variance):**
  > "The granting of a variance will not produce an undesirable change in the **character** of the neighborhood and will not unreasonably detrimentally affect the use or market value of abutting properties;"

## 2. Why these were not changed

Both tests track **30-A M.R.S. §4353**: §19.d.3 reproduces the four-prong **undue-hardship** standard (§4353(4)) — including the "essential character of the locality" prong — and §19.d.4 reproduces the **practical-difficulty / dimensional** standard (§4353(4-C)), including the "undesirable change in the character of the neighborhood" prong. The Code already cites the statute elsewhere in the same section (the §19.d.2 reference to the Disability Variance at "Title 30-A SS.4353.4-a"), which confirms the section is drafting *to* the statute. Rewording these prongs editorially would risk putting the ordinance out of conformity with the enabling act and is therefore a question for counsel, not the drafter.

## 3. The interpretive wrinkle the new definition creates

Because v0.20 now **defines "Character"** in Article 9, a literal reader could argue the Article 9 definition supplies the meaning of "essential character of the locality" in §19 — potentially **narrowing** a term that should carry its statutory/common-law meaning. That is almost certainly not intended.

**Suggested fix for counsel's consideration (a savings clause), e.g.:**
> "Terms used in standards quoted from or required by statute — including the variance standards in §19, which derive from 30-A M.R.S. §4353 — carry their meaning under that statute and the decisions construing it, and are not limited by the definitions in Article 9."

## 4. Questions for counsel

1. Confirm §19.d.3 and §19.d.4 conform to the **current** text and subsection lettering of 30-A M.R.S. §4353 (the Legislature has renumbered this section over time).
2. Confirm the "character" prongs should be **retained verbatim** (the drafter's assumption).
3. Advise whether a **savings clause** (¶3) should be added so the new Article 9 "Character" definition cannot be read to alter the statutory variance terms.

*This memo is issue-spotting by the code drafter for legal review; it is not legal advice.*
